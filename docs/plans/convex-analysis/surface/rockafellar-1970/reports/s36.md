## 2. The orientation convention — exact wording

The module docstring carries this verbatim, under the heading
`## The orientation convention (15449), in force for the whole rest of the book`:

> Rockafellar declares it in one sentence, immediately before Theorem 36.3, and never repeats it:
>
> > *"It is to be understood always that the minimization takes place in the convex argument of the
> > function, and that the maximization takes place in the concave argument."*
>
> For a **concave-convex** `K (u, v)` — concave in the **first** argument, convex in the
> **second**, which is §33's orientation and this Part's — that fixes both iterated extrema once
> and for all:
>
> | book | this module | reads |
> |---|---|---|
> | `sup_u inf_v K (u, v)` | `maximin K` | **maximise** over the **concave/first** argument, having **minimised** over the **convex/second** one |
> | `inf_v sup_u K (u, v)` | `minimax K` | **minimise** over the **convex/second** argument, having **maximised** over the **concave/first** one |
>
> Everything downstream is oriented by that single choice:
>
> * **Lemma 36.1** is `maximin K ≤ minimax K` — `sup inf ≤ inf sup`, never the reverse.
> * A **saddle-point** is a `p` with `K (u, p.2) ≤ K p ≤ K (p.1, v)` for all `u`, `v`: the
>   **first** argument is where `K` is *maximised*, the **second** where it is *minimised*.
> * `∂K = ∂₁K × ∂₂K` (§35, 15155) therefore mixes a **concave** subdifferential in the **first**
>   argument with a **convex** one in the **second**. That is why §37's Corollary 37.5.2 has to
>   insert `u* ↦ −u*` before it can speak of a monotone operator, and why Corollary 37.5.1's
>   homeomorphism is the asymmetric `(u − u*, v + v*)`.
> * The lower conjugate `K̲*` is `sup_v inf_u`, the upper `K̄*` is `inf_u sup_v` (§37,
>   15763/15769), and `K̲* ≤ K̄*` is Lemma 36.1 again (`lowerConjSaddle_le_upperConjSaddle`).
>
> **Theorems 36.3–36.6 and every result of §37 are false verbatim under the opposite convention.**
> Track the sign explicitly; do not try to derive it from the shape of a statement.
>
> For a *convex-concave* `K` the book exchanges the two words; the translation is plain
> **negation**, `convexConcave_lowerClosed_iff` / `convexConcave_upperClosed_iff` in §33 — *not*
> `saddleSwap`, which preserves the concave-convex class rather than flipping it.

* **`Saddle/Minimax.lean` already carries the whole of §36 under the book's labels**, including
  Theorem 36.6 in Kuhn–Tucker form and Theorem 36.5 in both directions, and its docstring records
  the same three design decisions the surface would have had to make (`HasSaddleValue` is not

  two-line consequence of `lowerAdjointBifun_eq_concaveAdjointBifun` plus `flip_pairing`, which is
  `Rockafellar.concaveAdjointBifun_inverseBifun`. Worth knowing before reaching for a proof.

* **`IsSaddlePoint`, `ClosedSaddleFn` and `SaddleEquiv` all unfold to `And`**, so dot notation on

  namespace out: `IsSaddlePoint.mem_domSaddle hp h`, `ClosedSaddleFn.saddleStructure hcl hK hp`,
  `SaddleEquiv.maximin_eq h`. Costs nothing once known; costs a confusing
  `The environment does not contain 'And.saddleStructure'` if not.

## 8. Gotchas worth adding to `gotchas.md`

**ER — `a - -b` on `EReal`: `sub_eq_add_neg` *does* fire, `neg_neg` alone does not.** Gotcha EL3
says `rw [neg_neg]` finds nothing in `a - -b` because `Sub` is a separate head, and suggests
`change`. There is a shorter fix: `EReal` is enough of a `SubNegMonoid` for Mathlib's generic
`sub_eq_add_neg` to apply, so `rw [sub_eq_add_neg, neg_neg]` closes it in one step. (What is *not*
available is `EReal.sub_neg`, which is the order lemma `a < b ↔ a - b < 0` and unifies against
almost anything — an easy wrong guess.) Hit in `saddleLagrangian_eq_inverseBifunBracket`, turning
`⟨u*, u⟩ - (F_* x)(u)` into `⟨u*, u⟩ + (Fu)(x)`.

**EL — `simpa using h` for a `ConvexFn`/`ConcaveFn` goal unfolds the predicate and then cannot
match.** `rw [concaveBifun_iff, concaveFn_iff_convexFn_neg]; simpa using h` reports the goal as
`Convex ℝ (epi fun x ↦ -graphFn (inverseBifun F) x)` against `h : ConvexBifun (flipBifun F)`:
`simp` unfolded `ConvexFn` to its `epi` definition on the goal side but left `h` bundled. Fix the
*function* first with a `funext` `have` (`(fun q => -(graphFn (inverseBifun F) q)) =
graphFn (flipBifun F)`, closed by `funext fun _ => neg_neg _`), `rw` that, and finish with `exact`.
Cost: ~10 minutes. Hit in `concaveBifun_inverseBifun`.

Nothing else in this section cost more than twenty minutes.
