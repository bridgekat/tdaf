## 5. Surprises

* **§37 is the largest section of Part VII and the thinnest surface in it.** Eighty-four
  declarations, seventy-eight of them one to three lines. The whole of §37 — including the
  unbounded minimax theorem, Theorem 37.5's four equivalent conditions, and both clauses of
  Corollary 37.5.1 — was already in the backbone under Rockafellar's own labels. The surface added
  no mathematics whatsoever; what it added is the book's *quantification* (Theorem 37.1's four
  equations as four statements, Theorem 37.5's four conditions as four `Iff`s against (d)) and the
  bridge from "closed proper concave-convex `K`" to "`K ∈ Ω (F)`".
* **The single most expensive thing in the section was hypothesis plumbing, not proof.** Four
  expressions account for 45 argument occurrences (§2 gap 6). Three sections in a row have now asked
  for a bundled closed-proper-saddle-function hypothesis; §37 is the one where the absence is
  measurable.
* **`Ω`'s definitional unfolding and `bifunSaddleClass` are not reducibly equal**, and that is the
  only thing in the whole section that produced a type error I could not read off the statement
  (§2 gap 8). `Ω` being an intersection with `{K | ConcaveConvexFn K}` — §34's deliberate and
  correct choice — is what puts a `Set.inter` between the two spellings.
* **`separatingRight_pairing` is in the shared header and `SeparatingLeft` is not**, even though
  every `C*`-side theorem of §37 asks for the left one and `Saddle/Existence.lean` is built entirely
  out of them. The asymmetry is invisible until a section touches the `C*` half of anything.
* **Theorem 37.2 is the only result in §37 whose backbone is asymmetric.** `Saddle/Conjugate.lean`

* **Corollary 37.1.1 needs no properness and Theorem 37.4's first sentence needs no hypothesis at

## 6. Gotchas worth adding to `gotchas.md`

**EL — `simpa … using h` fails where `exact h` would succeed, when the mismatch is a plain `def`.**
`simpa` closes with `assumption`-style matching at *reducible* transparency. A surface `abbrev`
(`subgrad`, `upperConj`, `pairing`) is reducible and is seen through; a backbone `def`
(`bifunSaddleClass`, `saddleOfBifun`, `bifunOfSaddle`) is not. The symptom is a "Type mismatch:
After simplification, term `h` has type … but is expected to have type …" naming two spellings that
are visibly the same object. The fix is a typed intermediate:
`have h' : <the goal's own spelling> := by simpa … using h; exact h'`. Used twice in
`Section37.lean` (`upperConj_mem_Ω`, `lowerConj_mem_Ω`). Cost: ~10 minutes. This is the third face
of §33's EL entry: reducible abbrevs block `rw`; plain `def`s block `simpa`.

**LINT — `show` that changes the goal is rejected.** `linter.style.show` now fires on a `show`
whose type is not syntactically the current goal ("The `show` tactic should only be used to indicate
intermediate goal states for readability… Please use `change` instead"), and it fails the
zero-warning bar. Any `show` used to unfold an `abbrev` must be `change`. Hit once in
`theorem_37_1_conj_upper`.

**DEP — `Set.mem_setOf_eq` is deprecated in favour of `Set.mem_ofPred_eq`.** A `simp only` list
carrying the old name builds but warns. Hit once in `corollary_37_5_2`.

**LIB — there is no `LinearMap.separatingRight_iff_flip_separatingLeft`.** Neither Mathlib nor
`Duality/Pairing.lean` has it (the latter's docstring says non-degeneracy is
`SeparatingLeft B ∧ SeparatingRight B` and introduces no predicate). For a self-paired Euclidean
space, prove `SeparatingLeft` directly: `fun _ hx => inner_self_eq_zero.1 (hx _)`. Guessing the
`iff` name cost ~10 minutes.

Nothing else in this section cost more than twenty minutes.
